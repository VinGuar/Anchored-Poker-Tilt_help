Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-26

Anchored ("we", "our", "us") provides a poker mental-performance coaching app. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data we collect

  • Account data: email address and optional display name.
  • App data: session history, check-in responses, and notes you create.
  • Technical data: basic diagnostics required for app operation.

How we use data

  • To provide account access and sync your data.
  • To power session tracking, insights, and coaching features.
  • To secure the service and prevent abuse.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We use infrastructure providers to host and process data required to operate the product.

Retention and deletion

You can request account deletion from the in-app Profile screen. Deletion requests remove account data associated with your user ID.
